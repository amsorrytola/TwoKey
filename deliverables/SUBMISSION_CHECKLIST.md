## 2. Prototype video

Target three minutes, under 20 MB. Record at 1920x1080.

Before recording:

1. `make reset` to clear the ledger and reseed the insurer
2. Start both processes: `make api` and `make ui`
3. In the console header, click **COMPACT** to switch it to **PRESENT**, which enlarges
   the whole interface for legibility on video
4. Run `make eval` beforehand so the Trust Report has figures to display

Recording sequence is in `USAGE.md` under "A 3-minute demo run".
